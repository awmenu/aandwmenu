The Canadian A&W menu is organised the same way you will see it on the board in the restaurant: burgers first, then chicken, breakfast, sides, drinks and kids options. The Burger Family is the heart of it all, a line of beef burgers named Mama, Papa, Grandpa, Teen, Mozza and Buddy, each one a different size and build of the same idea. Prices on this site are careful Canadian averages gathered for planning. Burger Comparison: Which One Should You Order?

If two burgers are calling your name, compare them on size first and toppings second. The Mama and Teen are the same weight class, but the Teen adds bacon, cheddar and Teen sauce, which makes it richer and saltier; choose the Mama for a cleaner, fresher taste and the Teen for the full classic experience. Neither is better in the abstract; they are better for different moods, and knowing that saves you from ordering by habit alone.

Cheese lovers should look at the Mozza Burger, where mozzarella and Mozza sauce give a milder, creamier melt than cheddar, and at the Cheddar Bacon Uncle Burger for a premium, steakhouse style build. Spice fans have the Spicy Chipotle Burger, mushroom fans have the Mushroom Swiss, and plant-based guests have a genuine seat at the table with the Beyond Meat Burger rather than a side-dish workaround. That breadth is a quiet strength of this menu: a group with very different tastes can all eat well in one stop.

A final practical tip: match the burger size to the side size. A Grandpa with a large poutine is a challenge meal, not a lunch, while a Buddy with onion rings can be a perfect light dinner. Balance across the whole tray, not just inside the bun, and every burger on this page becomes the right burger on the right day.

What is the difference between a Mama Burger and a Teen Burger?

Which A&W burger is the biggest?

The Grandpa Burger, with three beef patties, is the biggest standard burger in the Burger Family.

What is the cheapest burger at A&W Canada?

Are A&W beef patties raised without antibiotics?

A&W Canada is well known for serving beef raised without antibiotics and without added hormones or steroids, a policy the chain has promoted for many years.

Can I customise my burger?

Yes. Extra pickles, no onion, light sauce, extra cheese and lettuce wrap style builds are everyday requests at the counter, drive-thru and in the app.

Which burger is best for a first visit?

The Teen Burger. It is the signature build most Canadians recommend first, ideally with onion rings and a frosted mug of root beer.

Is there a vegetarian burger at A&W Canada?

Yes, the Beyond Meat Burger uses a plant-based patty with classic burger toppings and is available at most locations.

Do burger prices include a combo?

Listed prices on this page are for the burger alone as planning averages. Combos with fries and a drink cost more but less than buying each item separately.

Franchising followed quickly, and the little stand became one of the earliest restaurant chains in North America, built entirely on the strength of one drink done properly.
